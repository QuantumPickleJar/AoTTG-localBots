# M2 combat-arc-1: engagement and nape arc

## Field evidence

`AICadetsM2Watch6` records build M2-navdiag-3. At game time 166.36 the cadet acquired Titan 1006, launched and attached two map hooks, and reached a reported nape distance of 8.27. At 168.399 it dispatched `Attack(true)` at distance 7.70. At 168.719 it recorded `confirmed_damage=false`: no target damage callback had been observed by that immediate check. The old trace did not sample the human attack state, so it does **not** prove an attack animation played or that no later damage occurred. The subsequent Titan 1006 death at 285.901 was reported `by_cadet=false`; do not attribute that kill to the bot.

The watcher samples near 168.399 show the cadet releasing a hook while moving quickly, but status/target information is published approximately once per second and can lag. It does not establish why the attempted strike missed. The two supplied clips are approximately 57 seconds and 6.6 seconds; reviewed sampled frames show the cadet moving in streets, but do not provide a verified frame of this particular attack. Do not infer an animation or hit from the `Strike` FSM transition alone.

One established policy defect occurs at 163.818–163.858: after a collision-risk release with 11.5 units of progress toward the target, `PlanApproach` saw the hook not ready and the airborne recovery immediately abandoned that Titan, within 0.04 seconds. The script previously also checked nape attack range only after a two-leg traversal or on entering PlanApproach; a flyby during the first leg or coast could be missed.

## What changed

- On a still-valid target, an unavailable hook now waits up to two game seconds for retraction instead of immediately abandoning pursuit. Hold, target loss, low gas and host authority continue to take priority. Timeout uses existing recovery.
- While approaching, including map-ODM legs and the experimental Titan arc, the controller checks each fixed tick for a clear, in-range nape after collision safety checks. It releases hooks and dispatches the real `Human.Attack(true)` in AttackSetup. It checks the wall line of sight again before the input. Range alone is not a confirmed hit or even an observed swing.
- Experimental Titan tether arc uses the same physical action APIs as traversal, no position/velocity/resource writes. Within 45 units, a map linecast must be clear and `Physics.SphereCast` against Titans must return the **selected living Titan's exact ViewID**. Only then does the cadet aim and launch a left hook toward the nape. A hook is considered geometrically near the nape only if its reported position is within 22 units, and it is released if that grows beyond 24. This proximity observation is not proof of the attached collider's identity. It begins bounded tangential `Human.Move` plus real gas while checking map collision, target validity, hook, reserve, and a maximum 1.5-second arc. A valid attack window or an abort ends the arc early. Two arc launches are allowed per acquired target; fallback is existing map-anchor travel/recovery.
- `CombatArcStyle` is a public addon field at the top of WaveCadet: `Alternate` (default, opposite side on the second arc), `Left`, `Right`, or `Off` (existing map-only approach). A left or right input is a direction of attempted tangential steering, not proof of a full 360-degree orbit. F8 shows the loaded arc setting in the profile. `CombatArcSeconds` and `AttackVerifySeconds` are separately bounded. No M0/M1 movement source changes are needed.
- `attack_attempt` records the input request, position, nape, velocity, orientation, human state and current animation. `attack_state_observed` records an observed Human.State of `Attack`; sampling can miss short changes, and state alone does not prove damage. A non-damage result waits one second after release by default for callbacks. `target_damaged` and `titan_die` retain callback attribution. Damage from another actor is not credited to the cadet and does not prematurely end the cadet's observation window. `orbit_launch`, `orbit_attach`, `orbit_end`, radial dot/cross and `orbit_attack_window` distinguish actions from measured geometry. Watcher samples include human state and animation.

## Required isolated live trial

1. Restart AoTTG2 after installing **AICadetsWaveCadet.cl**, **AICadetsCommandPanel.cl**, and **AICadetsM2Watcher.cl**. Enable only those three for this M2 trial. F8 should show `M2-combat-arc-1` and `arc=Alternate`; the watcher header should say `combat-arc-1`. A stale F8 label or old watcher header means the game did not load the matching files.
2. Do **not** enable AICadetsTraversalReliable, AICadetsTraversal, AICadetsProbe, or the M1 watcher. Reliable Traversal is a separate one-shot M1 demonstration, not a dependency or a movement helper consumed by M2. It may create an M1 cadet or stop after its demo. Keep M0/M1 files as rollback but disabled. One M2 cadet is expected in this branch.
3. In a fresh private City 1 / Training / Waves lobby, use a single living Titan with open space first; leave default Engine ground steering and the Alternate arc. Issue Engage once. Record the watcher file. Watch for `orbit_probe`, `orbit_launch`, `orbit_attach`, `orbit_end`, `orbit_attack_window`, `attack_attempt`, `attack_state_observed`, `target_damaged`, and `titan_die`. Absence of `orbit_launch` plus `selected_titan_not_observed` would indicate the documented target query did not yield the selected Titan; examine layers, cast geometry and actual runtime instead of assuming an orbit occurred.
4. To test sides separately, change `CombatArcStyle = "Left";` and `"Right";` in the addon source, reload, and compare fresh lobbies. `"Off"` isolates the prior map-anchor approach. Compare radial dot/cross and positions; a small or zero angle is not a successful circle. Use visible video when possible for the first maneuver validation, and align it with game-time events. The watcher samples at 5 Hz and can miss short animation transitions.
5. Test a blocked nape and two-Titan scene: no hooks should launch through a map obstruction, and a different Titan returned by the cast must not become the selected target. Check Hold while attaching, low-gas diversion, target disappearance, and return to search. Verify any real hit by callback and HUD, not movement, target disappearance, or a fake health/position write.

## Limits and rollback

Tests are translated-source mocks, not an AoTTG2 interpreter, physics or live animation. This experiment has not been observed in-game: actual Titan cast layers, hook attachment behavior, tangential control, attack animation, damage, wave credit and late callbacks are unknown. A 1.5-second bounded arc may be only part of a turn. Targets near or behind buildings can make the query fail and use map traversal; no general city path planner is implied. If parsing or behavior regresses, restore the prior M2-navdiag-3 controller and watcher and disable the experiment. Never merge PR #6 until the live milestones pass.

Map `Physics.LineCast` remains the obstruction query.
- [`Titan.NapePosition`](https://github.com/AoTTG-2/CustomLogicDocumentation/blob/ded03751aee1b1179141aeb534faa2f1fc266aff/reference/Entities/Titan.md), Character `ViewID` and [`Character.CurrentAnimation`](https://github.com/AoTTG-2/CustomLogicDocumentation/blob/ded03751aee1b1179141aeb534faa2f1fc266aff/reference/Entities/Character.md) are documented observations. Actual runtime semantics require the trial above.
